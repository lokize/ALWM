# Nintendo Price Watcher

Watch Nintendo eShop prices via [Deku Deals](https://www.dekudeals.com/). Set a target price and get notified when it hits. Chip on the workspace bar shows deals on target.

## Usage

Enable in **Settings → Plugins**, click the chip. Search Switch games, pick eShop region and interval.

Data: `~/.config/alwm/plugins/dev.alwm.nintendo-price-watcher.json`
