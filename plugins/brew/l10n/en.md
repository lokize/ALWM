# Brew Outdated

Homebrew updates on the ALWM workspace bar.

- Chip shows how many formulae/casks are outdated (`3`)
- Click to list packages, refresh, upgrade one or all
- Needs `brew` on PATH (`/opt/homebrew` or `/usr/local`)

Enable under **Settings → Plugins**.
