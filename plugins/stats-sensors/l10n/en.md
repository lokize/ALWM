# Sensors

Stats-style **Sensors** chip on the ALWM workspace bar.

- Chip shows `50°` (primary / hottest die)
- Click opens a dense popover with grouped temperatures (CPU, GPU, PMU, Battery, …) and °C/°F toggle
- Optional alerts: on-screen notification when CPU/GPU cross your threshold

Enable under **Settings → Plugins**.
